Frequently Asked Questions

Search or filter through common questions regarding our process, terms, and technical execution.

A standard high-converting landing page is delivered within 7–10 business days. Full-stack web applications and complete brand systems typically take 2–3 weeks. We work with strict milestone sprint deliveries so you're never left wondering about status.
We provide iterative checkpoints at every phase (Wireframes → Design → Interactive Build). Minor tweaks and copy refinements are unlimited during the build phase. Our goal is 100% client satisfaction before any public launch.
We specialize in lightweight, ultra-fast modern web technologies: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS, clean semantic HTML5, and Headless APIs (Shopify, Stripe, Supabase). We strictly avoid heavy bloated CMS themes that slow down your conversion rates.
Standard fixed-scope projects operate on a 50% upfront deposit and 50% upon final sign-off and deployment. We accept direct Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS/UPI), Razorpay, Stripe, and major debit/credit cards with GST invoicing.
We prioritize high-efficiency asynchronous communication via dedicated WhatsApp/Slack channels, Loom video walkthroughs, and weekly scheduled milestone check-ins. You receive a live project board to track progress in real-time.
